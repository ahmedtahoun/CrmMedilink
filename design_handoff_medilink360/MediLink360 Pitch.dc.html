The problem today

Rollouts live in three places at once

Sales chases leads in WhatsApp, trainers keep their own sheet, and the CEO finds out a clinic stalled weeks later. Nothing is connected.

No single source of truth

Spreadsheets, chat threads and memory. Stages are inconsistent and updates get lost.

Handoffs slip through cracks

A signed clinic waits days before training starts. Nobody owns the gap between the two teams.

Leadership flies blind

No live view of the funnel, no idea which reps convert, no early warning on overdue deals.

MediLink360 puts the whole journey — lead → signed → trained → live — on one board every team shares.

It's fully clickable. Go ahead.
